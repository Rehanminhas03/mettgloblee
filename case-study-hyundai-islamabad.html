The operating challenge

Hyundai Islamabad already has established brand and product equity. MettGlobal's role is not to manufacture a “from zero” story; it is to connect local content, digital pathways and lead handling around showroom outcomes.

01 / CONTENT

Local market relevance

Coordinate Facebook, Instagram and YouTube content around launches, product stories, offers and showroom visibility.

02 / JOURNEY

Move beyond passive traffic

Direct campaigns toward model information, brochures, enquiries, test-drive intent and service actions rather than treating website visits as the finish line.

03 / LEADS

Design the handoff

Structure enquiry capture, CRM/Sheet entry, notification and follow-up so campaign response can later be measured through sales stages.

04 / PHYSICAL

Reinforce the local presence

Use outdoor and on-ground activity to support digital messaging, with evidence added to the case study as placements and activations are documented.

The connected customer journey

The official Hyundai Islamabad site already gives customers model information, brochure access, service booking and other conversion pathways. The marketing system should route qualified attention into those actions and then measure the handoff.
