The assignment

The source material describes a new local EV marketing presence that needed the fundamentals built together rather than as separate vendor tasks.

01 / BRAND FOUNDATION

Build the social presence

Instagram setup, visual direction, content cadence and Islamabad-focused audience planning around the EV line-up.

02 / ACQUISITION

Create demand for the EV3

Meta lead-generation activity used the JMEV EV3 as the flagship campaign model for local prospect acquisition.

03 / AUTOMATION

Make every enquiry trackable

Lead capture flowed into a Google Sheet / CRM process, notification and follow-up path rather than sitting as isolated form submissions.

04 / LOCAL VISIBILITY

Reinforce digital with physical presence

Outdoor activity and event activation were part of the wider plan, with exact placements and event evidence still awaiting publication-ready documentation.
