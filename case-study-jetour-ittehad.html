The brief

Jetour Ittehad needed more than isolated posts or ads. The operating problem was to create a recognizable Islamabad presence and make each enquiry trackable through follow-up.

01 / FOUNDATION

Launch the digital presence

Facebook and Instagram setup, brand direction, content cadence and an Islamabad-focused audience strategy.

02 / ACQUISITION

Generate qualified demand

Meta lead-generation activity around the Jetour T1 i-DM using Reel and image creative.

03 / HANDLING

Make leads actionable

A structured flow from enquiry to Google Sheet/CRM entry, notification, follow-up and sales outcome.

04 / PRESENCE

Connect online and offline

Billboard visibility and documented public activations reinforced the same brand story beyond social platforms.

WHAT WE REMOVED

No unsupported “#1 dealership”, ROI or cost-efficiency claims.

The source workbook included language such as “most-booking”, “cost-effective” and “strong ROI signal” without the comparative market data, spend, revenue or CPL required to support those claims publicly. We kept the evidence and removed the theatre.
