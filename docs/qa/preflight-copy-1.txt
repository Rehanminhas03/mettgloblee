03 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Clear answers.
Before the first call.

What clients usually want to know about scope, delivery, meetings, collaboration and how Mett Global works.

Who is Mett Global and when was it founded?
+

Mett Global was founded in 2025 and is based in Islamabad, Pakistan. It is led by founder Hammad Ayub, co-founder and COO Muhammad Ahmad Aamir, and Usman Rafiq, Director of U.S. Business Development.

What does Mett Global actually do?
+

We build, grow and automate businesses through four services: Digital Marketing & Growth, Web & Software Development (including custom CRMs), AI Automation & Content, and E-Commerce & Supply Chain. We combine them around a defined business problem.

Can you recover Amazon FBA, Walmart WFS and courier claims?
+

Yes. We file and follow up Amazon FBA reimbursement claims for lost, damaged and returned inventory, Walmart WFS reimbursements, courier and logistics claims for lost or damaged shipments, and audit courier invoices to recover overcharges.

How does an engagement usually start?
+

We begin with the constraint: what is not working, what outcome matters and what evidence is available. From there we recommend an audit, project, retainer or embedded-support model.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?
+

Yes. Mett Global is Pakistan-based with international delivery and a U.S. business-development presence. Remote collaboration is built into the operating model.

Can Mett Global work alongside our existing team or agency?
+

Yes. We can own a defined workstream, fill a specialist gap or work as embedded support without replacing an existing internal team or partner.

Can we start with an audit before committing to a larger project?
+

Yes. A focused diagnostic engagement can review a website, funnel, eCommerce operation, workflow or technical area and produce prioritized recommendations before implementation.

How can I book a meeting?
+

Use the meeting page to choose an online or in-person session, select a preferred date from the calendar and pick a time slot. The request is sent to contact@mettglobal.com and the team confirms availability by email.

How do you measure success?
+

Measures depend on the work: leads, conversion, response time, inventory accuracy, fulfillment health, process time saved, technical quality or another agreed KPI. We define the measurement before execution.

Do you guarantee rankings, revenue or campaign results?
+

No responsible provider can guarantee outcomes controlled by markets, platforms or customer behavior. We commit to clear scope, disciplined execution, transparent reporting and agreed performance measures.
