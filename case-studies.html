How we publish proof

A case study is only useful if the reader can tell what is measured, what is operational evidence and what is still awaiting data.

01

Verified metrics

Counts, percentages and outcomes are calculated only from documented campaign or pipeline data.

02

Evidence-led narrative

We separate work completed from claims about market impact we cannot independently establish.

03

No vanity ROI

We do not publish CPL, ROAS, revenue or rankings when the underlying financial data is unavailable.

04

Living case studies

Pages can be updated as more client-approved analytics, photography and outcomes become available.
