Build a prospecting system before hiring more salespeople

More outreach volume rarely fixes weak targeting, unclear qualification and inconsistent follow-up. Build the operating system before scaling the headcount.

METTGLOBAL FIELD GUIDE · LEAD GENERATION & SALES · 9 MIN READ
ICPSIGNALQUALIFYOUTREACHFOLLOW-UP

Define a realistic ICP

Specify industry, geography, company size, buying role and the business conditions that make your offer relevant. A broad list is not an ideal-customer profile.

Use buying signals

Prioritize prospects showing observable reasons to act: active advertising with weak landing pages, hiring, expansion, marketplace friction, outdated websites or a clear operational gap.

Qualify before personalizing

Confirm the business is a plausible fit before spending time on deep personalization. Keep a simple priority model such as hot, warm and cold based on signal strength and service fit.

Make outreach diagnostic

Lead with the specific issue you noticed and a relevant next step. Avoid sending the full service catalogue to everyone.

Own the next action

Every live opportunity needs an owner, next action and follow-up date in the CRM. Pipeline leakage often happens because nobody owns the next step.

Good prospecting is a sequence of qualification decisions, not a contest to send the most messages.
